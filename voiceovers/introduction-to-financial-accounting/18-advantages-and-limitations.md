# Voice-over script: Advantages & Limitations

Lesson page: `advantages-and-limitations.html`  
Length: about 1:37. Record one take per slide, matching the slides on the lesson page.

## Slide 1 · Hook (0:00–0:10)

The accounts say a business is worth fifty lakh rupees. A buyer offers one crore twenty lakh. So are the accounts wrong? Not quite. Let's see what accounting does well, and where it falls short.

## Slide 2 · Record and report (0:10–0:28)

First, what accounting gives you. Reliable records, so you don't depend on memory. The base for the profit and loss account and balance sheet. Information for owners, managers, lenders, staff and the government. And evidence that courts accept.

## Slide 3 · Use it (0:28–0:42)

And what you can do with it. Compare this year with past years. Plan budgets and make decisions. Calculate income tax and GST. And put a fair price on the business when selling it.

## Slide 4 · Limitations (0:42–1:05)

Now the limitations. It records only what's measurable in money. It relies on estimates, like an asset's useful life. Accountants' choices of method can affect profit. Assets stay at historical cost. Inflation is ignored. And accounts can be manipulated to look better, which is called window dressing.

## Slide 5 · Example (1:05–1:25)

Back to the buyer. The books show net assets of fifty lakh. But land bought years ago at old cost is now worth forty lakh more. And the brand and loyal customers, worth about thirty lakh to the buyer, aren't recorded at all. Fifty plus forty plus thirty: one crore twenty lakh.

## Slide 6 · Recap (1:25–1:37)

So, are the books wrong? No. They follow the rules: historical cost and money measurement. They just don't show market value or things like brand and talent. Accounts are powerful, but read them alongside other information.

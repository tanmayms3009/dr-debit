Key takeaways

30-second read
  • ✓An asset is a resource the business owns or controls that is expected to bring future benefits.
  • ✓Assets are tangible (machinery, furniture, vehicles) or intangible (goodwill, patents, trademarks, copyrights).
  • ✓A liability is an amount the business owes to outsiders and must pay in the future, such as creditors or a bank loan.
  • ✓Both are split into current (settled within 12 months or the operating cycle) and non-current (longer term).

Notes

Meanings and classification

Knowing accounting terms is the base for everything that follows. Two of the most important are assets and liabilities.

Assets

Resources owned or controlled by the business, used to generate future profits.

Tangible
Physical: equipment, buildings, vehicles, computers, furniture
Intangible
No physical form but valuable rights: goodwill, patents, trademarks, copyrights, designs. A brand is recorded only if it was purchased; a brand built in-house is not shown as an asset.

Liabilities

Amounts the business owes to outsiders, to be paid in the future.

Examples
Suppliers for goods bought on credit; a bank loan plus interest; outstanding expenses

Current assets

An asset is current if any of these apply:

  • It is expected to be sold or used up within the normal operating cycle
  • It is held mainly for trading
  • It is expected to be realised within 12 months after the reporting date
  • It is cash or a cash equivalent, unless restricted from being used to settle a liability for at least 12 months after the reporting date

All other assets are non-current, e.g. machinery held for long-term use.

Current liabilities

A liability is current if any of these apply:

  • It is expected to be settled within the normal operating cycle
  • It is held mainly for trading
  • It is due to be settled within 12 months after the reporting date
  • The company does not have an unconditional right to defer settlement for at least 12 months after the reporting date

All other liabilities are non-current, e.g. long-term loans and debentures.

Common mistakes

Where marks are lost

WrongAll assets are things you can touch.

RightIntangible assets like patents and goodwill have no physical form but are still assets.

WrongA 5-year loan is entirely non-current.

RightThe part due within the next 12 months is a current liability; only the rest is non-current.

WrongA famous brand the business built itself is shown as an asset.

RightOnly a purchased brand is recorded. A self-built brand is not shown.

WrongDebtors are a Real account because they are an asset.

RightDebtors are an asset, but they are persons, so it's a Personal account.

Notes

Who uses accounting, and why

Accounting information is used by two groups: people inside the organisation (internal users) and people outside it (external users).

Internal users, especially managers, get detailed reports designed for decision-making. This is the job of management accounting. External users mostly depend on the published financial statements.

Internal users

Owners / shareholders
Judge performance, how well resources are used, and the progress of their investment.
Managers
Plan, control and oversee operations using budget, production and feedback reports. Most important where ownership and management are separate.
Employees
Pay, perks, bonuses and job security depend on the firm's profits and future.

External users

Lenders, banks & suppliers
Creditworthiness: liquidity, debt levels and ability to repay.
Investors & potential investors
Profitability, growth and risk before investing.
Government
Tax collection, compliance with labour and company laws, and the economic impact of business.
Regulatory agencies
Tax authorities and the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), among others, check legal and financial compliance.
Customers
Especially in long-term contracts: is the business stable enough to keep supplying?
Researchers & the public
Study industries, employment and the business's impact on society.

Who wants to know what?

UserGroupMain question
Owners / shareholdersInternalIs the business profitable, and is my investment growing?
ManagersInternalAre we on budget? Where can we cut costs?
EmployeesInternalWill there be a bonus or raise? Is my job safe?
Banks and lendersExternalCan the business repay the loan and interest?
SuppliersExternalWill we be paid on time if we sell on credit?
InvestorsExternalIs it worth investing in, and how risky is it?
Government and tax authoritiesExternalIs the right tax being paid? Are laws being followed?
CustomersExternalWill this business still be supplying us next year?

Note: most textbooks class employees as internal users. A few list them as external, because they are not part of management. Follow your own textbook in exams.

Common mistakes

Where marks are lost

WrongManagers use the same published statements as outsiders.

RightManagers get detailed internal reports (budgets, production and feedback reports) through management accounting.

WrongThe Company Law Board regulates companies today.

RightIt was replaced by the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) in 2016.

WrongCustomers have no interest in a seller's accounts.

RightLong-term customers check financial stability to be sure supply will continue.

WrongOnly current shareholders are external users interested in profits.

RightPotential investors are external users too, deciding whether to invest.
